What your site is costing you — ABL Concrete —

These tweaks should help you land a few more clients through your website.

I'm Jarrett, your local Las Vegas Cinematic Web Designer. I went through ablconcretellc.com the way a homeowner who just got your name would: land on the page, see what you do, then call or book. The foundation is solid: twelve five-star reviews, a free-quote booking calendar for each of 11 services, a contact form, and Lighthouse scores of 100 for best practices and SEO. The leaks are in the details: the phone number is the last thing on the home page, the page is heavy on a slow-phone test, and the pictures have no descriptions.

Checked October 18, 2026

What I found

01

Your phone number is the last thing on the home page.

In your reply you wrote that calling or texting 702-675-0702 is the fastest way to reach you. On the site, the first screen reads “Expert Concrete Solutions Built to Last.” with a Free Consultation button that opens the Book Online page, and the header holds the logo and a menu icon, with no phone number. At 1440 pixels wide the “Phone: (702)675-0702” link sits 14,742 pixels down a 16,372-pixel page, below the 11 service cards, the gallery and the contact form. It is the only phone link on the 15 pages I loaded: the Book Online, Reviews, Projects Gallery and 11 service pages show the numbers as plain text, there is no text-message link anywhere, and the office number, (702) 981-3610, isn’t a link on any page.

02

On a slow-phone simulation, the home page takes 10 to 14 seconds to show its main picture.

I ran Lighthouse’s slow-phone simulation twice on the home page. The largest picture finished at 13.9 and 10.1 seconds, and the performance score was 46 and 44 out of 100. The page is about 4 MB with 99 images on it, and the logo alone is a 515 KB JPEG that Lighthouse says could be about 509 KB smaller in a modern format. On desktop settings the same test scored 87 and 77, with the main picture at 2.2 and 3.7 seconds. This is a lab simulation, not a test on a real phone.

03

Most pictures have no description, and 11 picture links have no name.

On the home page, 96 of 99 images have no alt text, and on the Projects Gallery page 142 of 144 have none. The 11 service pictures and the logo on the home page are links with no text a screen reader can read. The gallery shows 71 numbered pictures and only four carry a label: Retaining Wall, Front Yard Wall, Concrete Repair and Epoxy. So a search engine or a screen reader gets no description of what most of your work shows. Lighthouse scored accessibility 90 and also flagged a heading-order problem.

04

Pavers, turf, stucco and drainage are on your list, but have no page.

Your home page lists 14 services, including pavers and artificial turf, concrete steps, stucco and masonry, drainage and leveling, and irrigation. The Book Online page and the sitemap have 11 service pages, and none of them is for those. Three of your 12 reviews are for jobs with pavers: “Concrete Pavers Installed”, “Backyard Rock Removal & Step Pavers” and “Pergola Footings to Spec”. Each service page is one short paragraph, and its title is the service name plus “Abl Concrete Llc” with no “Las Vegas”, for example “Stamped Concrete | Abl Concrete Llc”. The home page title, “Concrete Patio Installation in Las Vegas”, names one of the 11.

05

“Licensed & Insured” has no license number next to it.

The home page says “Licensed & Insured”, and none of the 15 pages I loaded shows a Nevada contractor license number. In my searches, four of the shops that came back for concrete or pavers print theirs on their pages: VIP Concrete, Centurion Concrete Contractors, NV Custom Concrete and FreshStart Landscaping. A homeowner comparing contractors can look a number up. I didn’t look up your license.

06

Searching your name returns other names with your phone number.

When I searched “ABL Concrete LLC Las Vegas”, your own site wasn’t one of the four results. They were a directory listing that places ABL Concrete LLC in Henderson (your site says Las Vegas), a site called ABL Concrete Enterprises Inc. that lists (702) 675-0702, a BBB profile for A.B.L. Concrete Enterprises Inc. with the same number, and a Thumbtack listing. I couldn’t open the Enterprises site to check it, so I can’t say whether it’s yours. Either way, it’s what a customer sees when they look you up.

07

A few small mismatches.

I found no video on any of the 15 pages. The footer links Facebook twice, to the same profile, and TikTok, but not the YouTube channel your email signature links. The server also sends a header that declares the page’s language as Chinese (content-language: zh-CN) while the page itself says English. I can’t say whether any browser acts on that.

What’s working

A visitor can ask for a free quote three ways: a booking calendar for each of 11 services, a contact form on the home page and another on the Projects Gallery page, and a tap-to-call link. The Reviews page shows 12 five-star reviews, each with a first name and last initial and a job title. The home page code carries your phone number, city and ZIP, robots.txt allows every crawler except PetalBot, the sitemap returns 200 and there’s an llms.txt. Lighthouse lab scores on the home page: best practices 100 and SEO 100 on all four runs, accessibility 90.

Unmeasured

Rankings, visitor numbers, your Google listing and its reviews, your Facebook, TikTok and YouTube, whether visitors finish the booking calendar (I opened one calendar page), and how the pages look on a real phone: I tested page widths in a desktop browser only.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    AI crawlers aren’t blocked

    Your robots.txt blocks only PetalBot, so nothing there keeps GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot or CCBot from reading the site.

  • Found
    llms.txt is in place

    /llms.txt returns 200 as plain text and describes ABL Concrete LLC as serving Las Vegas and Nevada since 2010.

  • Found
    Business markup on the home page

    The home page code carries business markup with your name, phone number, city, state and ZIP code.

  • Found
    Sitemap

    /sitemap.xml returns 200 and lists the home, Reviews, Book Online, Projects Gallery and 11 service pages.

  • Gap
    No hours, rating or social profiles in the markup

    The markup has no opening hours, no rating and no links to your Facebook or TikTok.

  • Gap
    No review markup

    The Reviews page carries no structured data, so its 12 five-star reviews aren’t marked up as reviews.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 18, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn’t measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google’s AI answers say when asked directly. I didn’t ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows it.
  • How your pages feel on a real phone. My speed numbers are a lab simulation of a slow phone, and I didn’t test on a phone.

What I’d fix first

Put your phone number and a text link in the header, add your Nevada license number beside “Licensed & Insured”, shrink the logo and the gallery pictures so the page loads faster, describe the pictures, and give pavers, turf and your other listed services pages with “Las Vegas” in their titles. Then check how your name looks in a search. A short film of your crews at work near the top would help too. I can’t promise where it will place you.
