02

Three different answers to what your hours are

The business block printed on your pages gives your hours as 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, every day. Your footer gives a second set: Monday to Wednesday and Friday 7:00 AM to 12:00 AM, Thursday 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Saturday and Sunday 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Your air conditioning page answers a common question with a third: We are available 24/7 for urgent cooling repairs. A customer with a burst pipe at 9 PM gets three answers and no way to know which one runs the phones. Fix: print one hours list, the same words in the business block, the footer and the answers on the service pages.

03

The map pin in your homepage code sits in Georgia

The hidden business details in your homepage code (what Google reads for your name, address and hours) put your map pin at 33.897917, -84.316097. Those coordinates sit in Georgia. The street address in the same block reads 4690 W Post Rd #130, Las Vegas, NV 89118, and your office is in Las Vegas. Fix: set the pin to the coordinates of your Las Vegas address and check the result in Google’s Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results).

04

Your license number is on a competitor’s site, not on yours

Your about page says: Work with Las Vegas’s top-rated plumbing, heating and cooling company by the Better Business Bureau. We are licensed, bonded, and insured. No page checked prints the license number itself. A competitor’s comparison page lists your Nevada license as C-21 #0051275, active, with a $500,000 bid limit, checked November 12, 2026. Fix: print Nevada C-21 #0051275 on your home, about and contact pages, beside the address.

05

No video plays on your main pages

The home, air conditioning, financing and contact pages carry zero video in their code. On the pages checked, the only video link is the YouTube channel in your footer. A buyer deciding between you and a competitor reads about your crews but never sees them at work. Fix: put one real video on the home page and the air conditioning page, a technician on a real Las Vegas job, playing on its own.

06

Buyers who do not know your name yet do not find you

Your air conditioning page runs a full replacement section, headed Time to Replace? The End-of-Summer Question, and ends it with Ready to discuss AC replacement? Schedule Online or Call (725) 237-1727. The work is on the site. In the searches below, buyers asking for that work in Las Vegas were sent to other companies. Fix: add a dated quick answer at the top of the home page and each main service page, starting with air conditioning, so a search result has a current, direct answer to link to.

What I’d fix first

Fix the phone numbers first: finding 1, then the hours in finding 2. One number and one hours list, printed the same way everywhere, including the hidden details in your code.

The fixes, in the order to do them

1. Print one phone number everywhere

Surface: Header, business block, air conditioning installation page and every tap to call link.

Do this: Replace the installation page line with: Call the experts at (725) 237-1727 to schedule your ac installation today. Set the phone line in the business block and in the hidden business details to (725) 237-1727 as well. (Finding 1.)

Test it: Open the home, about, air conditioning, installation and contact pages in a private window and check that the same number shows on each page.

2. Print one hours list everywhere

Surface: Business block, footer, service page answers and the hidden business details in your code.

Do this: Print the footer list in the business block and in the hidden details, word for word: Mon - Wed & Fri: 7:00AM - 12:00AM. Thurs: 7:00AM - 11:00PM. Sat & Sun: 8:00AM - 12:00AM. Correct the 24/7 answer on the air conditioning page to match the list you keep. (Finding 2.)

Test it: Read the business block, the footer and the hidden details side by side and check that all three give the same hours.

3. Move the map pin to Las Vegas

Surface: The hidden business details in your homepage code (what Google reads for your name, address and hours).

Do this: Set the pin coordinates to the coordinates of 4690 W Post Rd #130, Las Vegas, NV 89118, and add an entry for air conditioning and heating service. (Finding 3.)

Test it: Paste your homepage address into Google’s Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) and check that your business shows in Las Vegas with no errors.

4. Print your license number

Surface: Home, about and contact pages, beside the address.

Do this: Add this line beside the address on each page: Nevada Contractors License C-21 #0051275 (Finding 4.)

Test it: Open the about page in a private window and check that the license line shows beside the address.

5. Point the robots file at your own sitemap

Surface: robots.txt.

Do this: Replace the sitemap line in robots.txt with: Sitemap: https://anytimeplumbingvegas.com/sitemap.xml

Test it: Open anytimeplumbingvegas.com/robots.txt and check that the sitemap line names your own domain.

6. Add a dated quick answer

Surface: Top of the home page and the air conditioning page.

Do this: Add this block near the top of each page: Anytime Plumbing, Heating & Cooling, Las Vegas. Plumbing, heating and air conditioning since 1982. Free estimates on equipment replacement. Call (725) 237-1727. Updated November 2026. (Finding 6.)

Test it: Open each page and check that the first screen says what you do, where you are and how to reach you, with the date.
