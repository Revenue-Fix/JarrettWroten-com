02

Your phone number and hours differ from place to place.

Your site and its Contact page give 702-747-8912, with hours of Monday to Friday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm and Saturday 10:00 am to 5:00 pm. XPEL's installer list, where you appear as “Avalos Window Tint”, gives (702) 294-8468, and so does Experience.com. A Chamber of Commerce listing shows Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. TintingSource matches your site's hours. A customer who finds you through XPEL's list is given a different number than one who finds your site. I haven't called either number.

03

The first photo takes about 25 seconds to finish on a slow-phone simulation.

On two Lighthouse runs (a lab simulation of a phone on a slow connection) the largest item on the first screen, the photo g-wagon-window-tinting-5-scaled.jpg, finished at 25.5 and 25.4 seconds, and the performance score was 50 and 49. First content showed at 12.8 seconds on both. The home page weighs about 3,900 KiB. Lighthouse estimates about 811 KiB could be saved by sending pictures at the size they're shown, and it lists 11 files that hold up the first paint. That is a lab number, not a measurement of your visitors.

04

The page asks for fonts from a staging address, and the browser blocks them.

On both Lighthouse runs the browser console logged 8 errors. Four of them are blocked requests for Hind and Roboto font files from st66cn1kw8-staging.wpdns.site, an address whose name suggests a copy of the site used for testing. The Lighthouse best-practices score was 75.

05

Your robots.txt and sitemap have loose ends.

The robots.txt line that points to your sitemap reads “Sitemap:- https://avalostint.com/sitemap.xml”, with a stray dash after the colon, and Lighthouse reports the file as not valid (1 error). The sitemap itself loads and lists 54 pages, but 4 of them return “Page not found”: three blog posts and one author page. The dates it gives are all between July 4 and July 8, 2025. I loaded all 54 on October 18, 2026.

06

Two Book Appointment buttons lead to two different forms.

The “Book Appointment” button in your header and side tab goes to a booking request page on app.tintwiz.com that asks for name, phone, preferred day and time. The “Book Your Appointment” button on your Book Appointment page goes to a Jotform form that asks for a $25 window tint deposit or a $50 paint protection film deposit, to be deducted from the total, and offers card, Apple Pay and Cash App Pay. The word “deposit” appears on none of the 50 pages I loaded. Both forms open. I didn't submit either.

07

The dated reviews on your Testimonials page are from 2017.

The three testimonials on your Testimonials page that show a date were posted on May 31, 2017, July 19, 2017 and November 25, 2017, from Facebook and Yelp. The others show no date. Review sites list you at 4.8 stars from 414 to 462 reviews, and one listing shows reviews dated as late as February 2026, so the newest proof sits on other sites, not yours.
