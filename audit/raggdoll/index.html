02

The headline takes about 8 seconds to appear on a slow-phone simulation.

On two Lighthouse runs (a lab simulation of a phone on a slow connection) the first content showed at 2.4 and 2.3 seconds, but the largest item, your main headline, came at 8.0 and 7.9 seconds, and the performance score was 40 both times. Lighthouse flags your style sheet and the Google Fonts request as blocking the first paint, an estimated 490 and 640 milliseconds. That is a lab number, not a measurement of your visitors.

03

The top of the page jumps while it loads.

Both Lighthouse runs measured a layout shift of 0.21, and the jump comes from the hero text block, the “Welcome To RaggDoll Motorsports” area at the top. Google treats 0.1 or less as good. That number measures how much of the page moves while it is still loading.

04

Google's scripts add most of the page's blocking time.

The home page loads two Google tag scripts, about 194 KiB and 190 KiB, and the Google Merchant widget pulls in roughly 290 KiB more from Google's servers. Lighthouse says third-party code blocked the page for 460 and 490 milliseconds of a 570 and 580 millisecond total blocking time. The page weighs about 1,900 KiB in all. I can't tell which of those tags you rely on.

05

Some buttons and colors trip accessibility checks.

Lighthouse scored accessibility 86 on both runs. Three product-card buttons have no accessible name, so a screen reader announces only “button”. The text on two of your pink buttons, “Shop Porsche 718” and “Join”, doesn't have enough contrast against the pink, and the footer heading “Categories” skips a heading level. Best practices scored 75, and Lighthouse lists third-party cookies set by Google's scripts among the causes.

06

“Track & Dyno Proven Performance” has no film behind it.

A scrolling banner on your home page says “Track & Dyno Proven Performance”, and your About page says 150+ Tuned 718s, but neither page has a video player: I found no video on either one. The home page has a “Follow Us” section linking to your Instagram, @raggdoll_motorsports. A short dyno pull or track run on the home page would back up the claim where people are deciding.
