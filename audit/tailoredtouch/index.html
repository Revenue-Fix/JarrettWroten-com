What I found

01

The first screen takes about 8 seconds to appear on a slow-phone simulation.

On two Lighthouse runs (a lab simulation of a phone on a slow connection) the first content showed at 8.0 seconds both times, your headline finished at 11.4 and 12.5 seconds, and the performance score was 57 both times. Lighthouse lists two of your style sheets as blocking the first paint, and Google Tag Manager code, about 180 KB, held up the page for about 260 milliseconds. The page itself is light, about 1.7 MB in all. That is a lab number, not a measurement of your visitors.

02

The main Book button is hard to read.

The button on the first screen, “Book a $100 Consultation”, has a text-to-background contrast of 3.72 to 1: indigo text on light blue at 14 pixels. The usual minimum for text that size is 4.5 to 1. Lighthouse scored accessibility 96 on both runs.

The home page shows three reviews labelled “Google Review”, and your page code says three reviews at 5.0. I didn't check your Google listing, so I can't say how those three compare with what Google shows.
