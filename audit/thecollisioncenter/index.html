What I found

01

Your work is photographed, never filmed

Your gallery shows 31 real jobs, including a Bentley Arnage and a 2012 Maserati Granturismo front-end repair. Not one video plays anywhere on your site: 0 video elements or embeds on the pages checked, home, about, services, work, contact, reviews, free estimate, mechanical repair and accident restoration. Fix: put the gallery in motion, with short clips of the same jobs playing on their own where the stills sit now.

02

Your homepage takes 21.9 seconds to show its main content on a phone, in lab testing

PageSpeed's lab test put the time for your homepage's largest content to appear on a phone at 21.9 seconds, on about 4 MB of page weight, with no field data. The same test on a desktop took 2.1 seconds. Fix: cut the homepage weight first, starting with the slideshow images, and load the sections below the first screen only when a visitor scrolls to them.

03

Your footer still closes you on Saturdays in August 2026

The footer of every page still prints: "Please note that we will be closed on Saturdays August 2026." August is long over, and the note sits beside your hours, Mon to Fri, 8AM to 5:30PM. Fix: take the note down, or swap in the current holiday hours.

04

Three doors, all the same size

Free Estimate repeats everywhere, an equally prominent phone pill sits in the top bar of every page, and a separate contact form competes with both. A visitor gets three next steps, not one, on every page. Fix: pick one main next step per page, Free Estimate, and set the phone number beside it as the backup.

What I'd fix first

Fix the footer note and the square footage first (findings 3 and 5, minutes each). Then pick one next step per page (finding 4), then cut the homepage weight (finding 2).

The fixes, in the order to do them

1. Take down the August note

Surface: Footer, every page.

Do this: Delete this line from the footer: Please note that we will be closed on Saturdays August 2026. (Finding 3.)

Test it: Reload the homepage, scroll to the footer, and check that the hours show with no August line.

2. Correct the square footage

Surface: Homepage, the Finest Automotive Repair section.

Do this: Change "over 10,000k sq ft of working space" to: over 10,000 sq ft of working space (Finding 5.)

Test it: Search the homepage text for "10,000k" and check that nothing comes back.

3. Pick one next step

Surface: Top bar and page headers, every page.

Do this: Keep Free Estimate as the one main button in the top bar, and print 702-472-7777 as plain text beside it. The contact form stays linked in the menu. (Finding 4.)

Test it: Open the homepage, the work page and the contact page in a private window and check that each shows one main button.

4. Put the quick answer at the top

Surface: Homepage, under the headline.

Do this: Add this block under the headline: Collision repair and auto body in Las Vegas. The Collision Center, 2741 E Tropicana Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89121. Free estimates: 702-472-7777. Updated November 2026. (Finding 6.)

Test it: Open the homepage in a private window and check that the block shows on the first screen, with the date.

5. Cut the homepage weight

Surface: Homepage slideshow and below-screen sections.

Do this: Compress the slideshow images, and set the sections below the first screen to load only when a visitor scrolls to them. (Finding 2.)

Test it: Run your homepage through PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev) and check that the phone Largest Contentful Paint time drops below 21.9 seconds.

6. Ask for the review

Surface: Homepage, under the customer reviews.

Do this: Add this line under the reviews and link it to your Google review page: Had your car repaired here? A Google review helps the next Las Vegas driver find us.

Test it: Click the line on the homepage and check that it opens your Google listing.
