What I found

01

Buyers who search for what you sell land on your competitors

In 4 service searches a customer types in Las Vegas, Top Tech was not named and your site was not linked. The Cooling Company took every result in the installation search. Your site was linked as a source in 1 of the 5 searches, and Top Tech was named in 1 of the 5: your own name search. Fix: fix the business details in finding 2 first, then give the replacement search its own dated page under DIY 5.

02

Your homepage code carries two different phone numbers

The hidden business details in your homepage code (what Google reads for your name, address and hours) carry two different phone numbers for Top Tech: (702) 900-7540 in one entry and (702) 660-7506 in another. Every button and heading on your pages uses (702) 660-7506, and your name search listing gives the (702) 900-7540 number, the one your pages never show. Fix: keep one number in those details, the (702) 660-7506 your pages use.

03

Your appointment form asks for a street address before a question

Your main Request an Appointment form asks for a street address, a city and a zip code, plus a property type and a relationship to property answer, before its optional comment box. The lighter Send Us a Message form sits under it on the same page and asks only for a full name, a phone number and an email address, with the same comment box. Fix: lead with the message form, and move the address and sorting questions to the booking step after a reply.

Do it yourself

The fixes, in the order to do them

1. Keep one phone number in your business details

Surface: The hidden business details in your homepage code (what Google reads for your name, address and hours).

Do this: Remove the (702) 900-7540 entry from those details and keep (702) 660-7506, the number every page and button already uses. (Finding 2.)

Test it: Paste your homepage address into Google's Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) and check that one phone number shows.

2. Lead with the light form

Surface: Contact page.

Do this: Move the Send Us a Message form above Request an Appointment on the page, and rename the appointment form heading to: Book a visit (after we reply) (Finding 3.)

Test it: Open the contact page in a private window and check that the first form asks only for a name, a phone number and an email address.

3. Put a count beside the stars

Surface: Homepage, first screen.

Do this: Change the rating line to carry the review count from your own Google listing, in this shape: Rated 5.0 stars on Google, from [your Google review count] reviews Fill in the count from your Google listing on the day you publish it. (Finding 1.)

Test it: Open your Google listing and check that the count on the homepage matches it.

4. Film one job

Surface: Homepage and installation page.

Do this: Film one real installation from arrival to startup on a phone, in one take per step, and place the finished video under the first screen on both pages, set to play on its own. (Finding 4.)

Test it: Load the homepage in a private window and check that the video starts without a tap.

5. Add the dated quick answer

Surface: Homepage and each main service page, near the top.

Do this: Add this block near the top of each page and update its date when the page changes: Top Tech Air Conditioning & Heating, Las Vegas and Henderson. Full system replacements with Wells Fargo financing and free estimates on equipment installations. Call (702) 660-7506. Updated November 2026. (Finding 5.)

Test it: Open each page and check that the first screen says what, where and how to call, with the date.

6. Correct the four small defects

Surface: Financing page, homepage photo, site code and contact page.

Do this: Change the financing sentence to: Top Tech Air Conditioning and Heating Services has teamed up with Wells Fargo Look up your license on the Nevada State Contractors Board site and print that one number in the footer and on the van. Point the Facebook link at https://www.facebook.com/Toptechnv/ with nothing after it. Add a main headline to the contact page: Contact Top Tech Air Conditioning & Heating (Finding 6.)

Test it: Read the financing page, click the Facebook icon, and open the contact page, checking each fix in turn.
