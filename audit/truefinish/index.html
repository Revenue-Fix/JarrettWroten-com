02

Your llms.txt lists a different menu and different prices.

The llms.txt file describes packages that aren't on your page: Interior Only at $65, Exterior Only at $65, Full Interior Detail at $115 and Complete Detail at $160, with size surcharges. Your page shows The Basics at $95, The Premiere at $145, The Executive at $215 and ceramic coating from $675, and the llms.txt doesn't mention ceramic coating. An AI assistant that reads llms.txt can quote the old prices.

03

Your home page says 500+ reviews, and the listings I found show far fewer.

Your reviews section says “5.0 | 500+ Reviews”. The listings I found show 6 reviews at 5.0 on one and 11 at 4.6 on another. I can't see where 500+ is counted, and I didn't check your Google listing, so I can't say how it compares. A visitor who checks may notice the gap.

04

The main Book button is hard to read, and phones can't zoom.

The “Book Your Detail” button on the first screen is white text on sky blue (#0ea5e9), a contrast of 2.77 to 1 against the 4.5 to 1 minimum. The viewport setting also includes maximum-scale=1, which stops pinch-zoom on a phone, and the Instagram icon in the footer is a link with no readable name. Lighthouse scored accessibility 86.

05

Your service area doesn't name Pahrump, and the listings don't agree on where you are.

The page, markup and llms.txt name Las Vegas, Henderson and other valley areas, and the word Pahrump appears nowhere on the page. One listing puts you at 985 Wigwam Pkwy in Henderson, another gives only Las Vegas, and your own page gives no address. In a search for mobile car detailing in Pahrump NV, the search named JJW Detailing, Barnacle Brian's, Diamond Detailz and MoreThanJustAWash, not you.

06

I found no video on the page.

The page has a gallery of project photos but no video player, and your Instagram, @Truefinishdetailing, is linked in the footer. A short clip of a wash or a polish near the top would show the work where people are deciding.
