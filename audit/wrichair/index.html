Six findings, in the order a buyer hits them

01

Your site gives two different sets of hours on the same page

One part of your site says you are open every day, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Another part of the same page says Monday to Friday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Sunday CLOSED. The same clash repeats on your service pages and your About page. A buyer who checks on a Sunday gets both answers at once.

Fix: pick the one true set of hours and put that same set in both places, word for word.

Your hours, block oneOpen every day
Every day: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

That includes Sunday.

Your hours, block twoA different answer
Monday to Friday: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Your hours, block twoSunday: CLOSED

Same page. Two answers. The buyer picks one and hopes.

02

Not one video on the homepage of a company working since 1992

Your homepage carries no video at all. No clip of a technician at work, no walk through of a replacement, nothing moving that shows the crew a buyer is about to let into their home.

Fix: put one real work clip on the homepage, shot on a real job.

03

Your site prints no street address and no email address

Every contact block on your site gives the phone number and one line for location: Las Vegas, NV. No street address, and no email address anywhere on the site. Listings elsewhere carry a street address for you: 1517 Merialdo Ln, Las Vegas, NV 89117, checked November 12, 2026. Your own site is the one place that leaves it out.

Fix: add the street address to every contact block, and add one email address beside the phone number.

04

Your license number is nowhere on your own site

No page on your site prints your license number. A competitor's comparison page lists your Nevada license as #0034650 (C-21), Active, checked November 12, 2026. A buyer who wants to check you has to leave your site to find it.

Fix: print the license number in the footer of every page, beside the phone number.

05

No date sits beside any opening answer

Your home page opens with no date, and no main service page puts a date beside its opening answer. A buyer cannot tell whether the page in front of them is current.

Fix: add one line under each opening answer, in this form: Updated November 2026, with the real month of the last change to that page.

06

Small cleanup: the footer copyright still ends at 2024

The footer copyright on your site still ends at 2024. It is a small line, and it is the last thing on every page. A two year old date at the foot of the site undercuts the current, working company the rest of the page describes.

Fix: update the footer year to the current year, and set it to roll over on its own each January.

The fixes, in the order to do them

1. Make the hours agree

Surface: Every page, both hours blocks.

Do this: Choose the true hours, then replace both blocks with that one set, word for word. The set on your visible hours block reads: Monday to Friday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Sunday closed (Finding 1.)

Test it: Open the homepage in a private window, scroll the full page, and check that every hours line matches.

2. Print the address, email and license

Surface: Contact blocks and footer, every page.

Do this: Add this line to every contact block: 1517 Merialdo Ln, Las Vegas, NV 89117 Add your email address beside the phone number, and add your Nevada license #0034650 (C-21) to the footer. (Findings 3 and 4.)

Test it: Open one service page in a private window and check that the address, email and license all show without hunting.

3. Put one real work clip on the homepage

Surface: Homepage, under the opening section.

Do this: Film one real replacement or repair job on a phone, keep it under a minute, and place it under the opening section with the line: See us in action (Finding 2.)

Test it: Load the homepage and check that the clip plays and shows your own crew and truck.

4. Date the opening answers

Surface: Home page and each main service page, under the opening answer.

Do this: Add one dated line under each opening answer: Updated November 2026 Use the month of the real last change on each page, and update the line whenever the page changes. (Finding 5.)

Test it: Open each service page and check that a date shows on the first screen.

5. Bring the footer current

Surface: Footer, every page.

Do this: Change the footer copyright end year from 2024 to the current year, and set it to update on its own each January. (Finding 6.)

Test it: Scroll to the foot of the homepage and check the year.
