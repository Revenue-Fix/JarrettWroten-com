I

Speed To Lead

Win the leads that would have gone to whoever answered first.

When someone reaches out, the business that answers first usually gets the job. So every call, text and form gets an answer right away, even when you’re busy with a customer.

  • The calls you can’t pick up get answered
  • Every missed call gets a text back in seconds
  • Every text and form gets a fast reply
  • Each lead is written into your own system, so you can call them back while they still want you

II

Nurture

Close the ones who weren’t ready yet.

Plenty of people don’t book the first time they reach out. Nurture keeps you in front of them with text and email follow-up until they book or tell you no, so a good lead doesn’t go cold.

  • Text and email follow-up, set up for you
  • It stops the moment someone books
  • Every reply comes straight to you

III

Re-Activation

Turn your old list into this month’s revenue.

You already paid for the leads sitting in your phone and your system. In the first 30 days, I work that old list for you, so your first booked calls can come from people who already know you.

  • Missed callers who never got a call back
  • Quotes that never booked
  • Past customers who are due again

IV

Book The Call

Every touch ends with a time on your calendar.

Every answer, every follow-up and every old-list message ends by asking them to book. Booking takes one tap, and the confirmations and reminders are there to get them to show up.

  • One-tap booking that puts the job on your calendar
  • A confirmation the moment they book
  • A reminder text the day before
  • A new time offered when someone misses
  • A review request after every finished job

One System, All Four Pieces

Get Booked is one full system, never sold in pieces. A fast answer with no follow-up, or a follow-up that never asks for the booking, still lets the job walk. Each piece hands the lead to the next one, until the lead is a booked call that shows.

Getting every lead on the phone with you is what it’s built to do. I can’t promise how many will book, so you see your real numbers every month.

Example: A Car Dealership

Example

Here’s how the four pieces fit a car lot.

  • Speed To Lead. An internet lead asks about a truck after the desk has gone home, and gets an answer right away with a time to come see it.
  • Nurture. A shopper who wasn’t ready gets follow-up until they book a test drive or say no.
  • Re-Activation. The old leads in the store’s system get worked for buyers who never came in and past buyers who are due again.
  • Book The Call. Each one ends with an appointment on the store’s calendar and a reminder the day before.

The goal at a store is the same as anywhere: more appointments that show, and more cars sold.
