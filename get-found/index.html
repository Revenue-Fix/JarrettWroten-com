The First 30 Days

Before any work starts, I ask ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask, several times each, and save screenshots next to your top competitors. Then I fix:

  • Your Google Business Profile
  • The listings AI tools pull from
  • Your service pages
  • The code that tells Google and AI answers what you do and where

I also pitch you for best-of lists and local mentions. At day 30 I ask the same questions again, so you see the before and after side by side.

Your Google Business Profile

Your profile is one of the first places Google and AI answers look.

  • Your name, address, phone, website and hours match on your site, your profile, your booking tool and your listings
  • The right categories, services, description and links
  • Your website link tagged, so you can see the visits your profile sends
  • Your Book button pointed where it should go
  • The same name and address on Apple Maps

Changes that can make Google re-verify you, like a new address or keywords in your business name, wait for your yes.

Every Month After
