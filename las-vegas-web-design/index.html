Before You Start

A Few Useful Questions.

Do I Need A Full Redesign?

A focused fix may be enough if the site still explains your business well. A redesign is worth considering when your services have changed, your work is hard to see, or the mobile experience makes contacting you difficult.

What Affects The Cost?

The number and complexity of pages, available copy and images, your existing platform, and any forms, booking tools, or CRM connections. We agree on scope and price before paid work begins.

What Happens After The Site Is Built?

Ongoing support is separately scoped. Any updates, maintenance, or follow-up work we agree to include will be defined with the project.

What Is The Free Concept?

It’s a design direction for your website to discuss on the call. Add your current site when you book so I can prepare it beforehand. If you prefer an initial look by email, send your site and what you want it to do better.
