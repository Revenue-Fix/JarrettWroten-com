Privacy

Here Is What Happens ToThe InformationYou Send Me.

If you email me, text me, send the contact form or book a call, I use what you send to answer you, get ready for the call, and follow up.

What I Use Who Receives It Text Messages How Long I Keep It Your Choice What Not To Send Tracking

What I Use

I use your name, email, phone number, message and booking details for your request.

Who Receives It

I receive what you send. When you book, Google Calendar also receives the details you enter. When you send the contact form, FormSubmit delivers it to my email. When I text you, my texts go through Quo, my business phone service.

Text Messages

If you give me your mobile number and agree to texts, I text you about your request: answers to your message, call times and updates on your project. How often depends on your project.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text from me to opt out, and I will stop texting you.

I never sell or share your mobile number or your texting consent with anyone for their marketing or promotions.

How Long I Keep It

I keep it while I need it to handle your request and keep normal business records.

Your Choice

Email Jarrett@JarrettWroten.com if you want me to fix or delete your information.

What Not To Send

Do not send sensitive information by email, text, the contact form or your booking notes.

Tracking

This site remembers your motion preference in this browser. It uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visits and see how people move through the pages, so I can make the site easier to use. These tools may set cookies and collect things like pages viewed, device type and approximate location. They do not see what you type into forms. Email me if you have questions.
