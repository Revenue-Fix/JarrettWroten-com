Here Is What Happens To The Information You Send Me.

If you email me, send me a message through my site or book a call, I use what you send to answer you, get ready for the call, and follow up.

What I Use

I use your email and booking details for your request.

Who Receives It

I receive what you send. When you book, Google Calendar also receives the details you enter. When you send a message through my site, FormSubmit receives it and emails it to me.

How Long I Keep It

I keep it while I need it to handle your request and keep normal business records.

Your Choice

Email Jarrett@JarrettWroten.com if you want me to fix or delete your information.

What Not To Send

Do not send sensitive information by email, in a message through my site or in your booking notes.

Tracking

Some pages, like the audit pages and the Dylan Prorok project page, remember your motion preference in this browser. It uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visits and see how people move through the pages, so I can make the site easier to use. When you click a call, email or booking link, that click is recorded so I can see which pages bring in calls. These tools may set cookies and collect things like pages viewed, device type and approximate location. They do not see what you type into forms. Email me if you have questions.
