Restaurant Concept

Pā‘ina Café

Pā‘ina means gathering. The kitchen in motion becomes the invitation.

The organizing idea

Gathering.

Kitchen.
Handoff.

The concept does not treat mochi, poke, and prepared food as separate menu boxes. It treats them as parts of one gathering: food being made, finished, and handed to someone.

That gave the site a reason to move. The opening holds three kitchen rhythms together on desktop, then becomes a single portrait sequence on mobile so the story stays legible instead of shrinking into three tiny windows.

The experience

One invitation, built from three kinds of motion.

Mochi

Rhythm

Mixing, forming, and frying make craft visible before a visitor reads a claim about freshness.

Poke

Color

Assembly gives the middle of the passage contrast and makes the choice feel immediate.

Takeout

Handoff

The story ends with food leaving the kitchen, giving the order action a destination instead of dropping it onto the page.

Restaurant Concept

The kitchen becomes the invitation.

I built this concept around food moving from the kitchen into someone's hands. That motion is the idea I would bring to Pā‘ina Café.

It is a restaurant concept, so the invitation itself is what I am here to show.
