Rana Levy

A jewelry experience built around the hand, the cut, and the way a stone changes in the light.

The material leads

Make the change in the stone visible.

Rana Levy cuts stones by hand. A flat catalog treatment would hide the thing that makes the work distinct, so the concept lets the studio, the hand, and the changing color carry the first part of the story.

The gem is not used as decoration around an ecommerce template. It becomes the transition itself, moving the visitor from the maker's world into the decision they came to make.

Information architecture

Three intentions. Three clear paths.

Ready Now

For the visitor who wants to see finished pieces and decide from what is available.

Made To Order

For someone drawn to a specific form who wants a piece created to order.

Custom Consultation

For a customer whose decision begins with a stone, a story, or a conversation.
